# Yassine KCHAIT 👋
Building free, useful things for Morocco with AI. Everything below is open source, runs on $0 infrastructure, and was built with local AI tools.

## 🌍 [Outils.ma](https://hakersbkk.github.io/outils-maroc/)
Free calculators, converters & practical guides for Morocco — in French & darija.
Tools: IMC, TVA (20/14/10/7%), MAD⇄EUR live rates, salary net⇄brut estimation, loan simulator, calories, age, shoe sizes, password & QR generators… plus practical Moroccan life guides.
*Code: [outils-maroc](https://github.com/Hakersbkk/outils-maroc) · grows automatically with new pages*

## 🏪 [PageSprint](https://hakersbkk.github.io/pagesprint/)
One-page websites for local businesses, delivered in 24h — AI-built from an OpenAI-compatible local gateway, MIT templates included.
*Code: [pagesprint](https://github.com/Hakersbkk/pagesprint)*

## ⚡ Amal — AI assistant demo
A live chatbot that answers customers in darija, Arabic, French & English: quotes prices, explains delivery, takes orders. Try it: [demo](https://hakersbkk.github.io/pagesprint/#chatbot-demo) *(live instance runs on my own machine; ask me!)*
